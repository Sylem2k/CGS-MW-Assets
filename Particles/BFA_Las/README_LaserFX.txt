Laser visual prototype: BFA_LasBolt_Trail.ptc is a lightweight cyan streak made for Mech Core's moving local projectile-trail entity. It is NOT a full continuous instant beam. Both emitters max 256 particles each. Test Workbench import, visual persistence and multiplayer with the paired MW Gameplay branch. BFA_LasBolt_Hit.ptc and BFA_LasBolt_Hit_API.ptc are separate impact effects; do not use those as projectile trails.
